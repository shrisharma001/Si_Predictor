    Before you rely on a prediction

    The model was trained on one furnace. Predictions follow the previous lab results closely and underestimate upsets: when silicon rises above about 1.2 wt% the interval usually misses. Intervals are also less reliable when the last lab result is more than a few hours old. Use the prediction as a guide next to the lab result, not as a control setpoint.
